# Pattern: Accommodation-Resentment (Cross-Domain)

**First observed:** 2026-04-19 (session 4, Cyrus context)
**Cross-domain confirmation:** 2026-05-05 (session 10, workplace)
**Vertical configuration added:** 2026-05-18 (session 11, workplace hierarchy)

## Description

Yinnie absorbs relational cost to keep situations functional, does not receive acknowledgement for the cost, and builds resentment. This is not passive - she is making an active choice to prioritise diplomacy and forward motion over claiming accuracy or expressing her actual position. The cost is invisible to others, and the lack of acknowledgement compounds into frustration.

## Cross-Domain Instances

**Romantic (Cyrus):**
- October incident: verbally abused by estate manager, Cyrus did not intervene or check in. She absorbed the hurt, did not make it a dealbreaker, resentment built.
- Holiday planning: his friends' travel collapsed her alone time. She pre-calculated his objection rather than stating her need directly. (Session 8 - she eventually asked, he delivered. Session 9.)
- General pattern: she describes herself as always having to ask, carrying the relational weight.

**Workplace - lateral (Ant, Sam):**
- Ant was publicly rude, she initiated a direct conversation, kept her actual position out of it ("knowing I am right"), moved things forward diplomatically. He apologised. She carried the cost of not being acknowledged as right.
- Sam went to CTO behind her back, CTO asked her to yield, she did. Sam vented to Ant for an hour about a PR instead of talking to her.
- "Why do I always have to bend over backwards for these people?"

**Workplace - vertical (allocation project, session 11):**
- Product committee narrowed scope above her. She delivered that decision downward to Rob. Rob escalated to Joe (his manager) instead of coming back to her. Joe put in a meeting with her. She now absorbs scrutiny for a decision that was not hers.
- This is a squeeze from both directions: institutional decisions flow through her, and the people affected blame her rather than the institution.
- Rob going to Joe instead of talking to Yinnie directly is structurally identical to Sam going to the CTO instead of talking to her. She has not made this connection.

**Workplace (structural):**
- "Being a PM is just that's the way it is" - the role itself requires absorbing relational friction by design.

## Relationship to Other Patterns

This pattern is the behavioural mechanism that feeds the reset-and-resentment cycle (Cyrus-specific) and maintains the PM fatigue (work-specific). It connects to the mirrored people-pleasing observation: she criticises others for people-pleasing or not communicating directly while herself accommodating silently.

The "maybe I should be meaner" conclusion (session 10) is a misidentification of the problem. She has the skill for directness - the Ant conversation proves it. The actual gap is not assertiveness but the recognition that her relational labour is invisible and the willingness to make it visible.

The dispositional attribution pattern compounds this: she absorbs the cost AND blames herself for the situation that created it. In session 11, every element of the allocation situation routed through "I'm a bad PM" rather than "the committee made a decision and I carried it."

## Clinical Notes

The cross-domain and now vertical nature confirms this as a characterological pattern, not a situational response. It is likely maintained by: (1) genuine skill at diplomacy - she is good at this, which means it gets reinforced; (2) identity as someone who keeps things moving; (3) fear that claiming her position will make her "the annoying one"; (4) the PM role structurally rewarding exactly this behaviour; (5) the dispositional attribution pattern that turns the resentment inward rather than outward.

The resentment accumulation is a maintaining factor for the chronic low mood. She is carrying unacknowledged costs across multiple domains simultaneously.

## Watch For

- "Why do I always have to..." or "bending over backwards" - the resentment surfacing.
- "Maybe I should be meaner/tougher" - misidentification. The gap is visibility of cost, not intensity of response.
- New instances of people routing around her (Rob to Joe, Sam to CTO) - this is a recurring structure.
- Whether she begins to recognise the cross-domain parallel.
- Whether she can articulate what acknowledgement would look like.
- The vertical squeeze: committee decisions flowing through her and landing as blame from below.
